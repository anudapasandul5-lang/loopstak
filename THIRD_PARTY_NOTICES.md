# Third-party notices

Some interface patterns on this site are adapted from open-source component libraries. They were rebuilt in this project's own CSS and code (no package is installed), but the ideas and some values come from the works below, so their licences are reproduced here.

## SmoothUI

Source: https://github.com/educlopez/smoothui (https://smoothui.dev)

Patterns adapted:
- Animated Tabs: the sliding marker under the price tabs (`src/components/PriceTabs.tsx`) and the sliding pill in the LKR/USD switch (`src/components/ListToggle.tsx`).
- Price Flow: digits that roll in when the price list changes (`src/components/Price.tsx`, `.price-amt .ch` in `globals.css`).
- Basic Accordion: the smooth open and close of the FAQ.
- Glow Hover Card: the mint light that follows the pointer along card borders (`[data-glow]`).
- Infinite Slider: the Services ticker pauses on hover.
