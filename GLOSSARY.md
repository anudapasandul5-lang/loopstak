# Loopstak

The words used on the Loopstak site and in its planning. Loopstak sells websites and AI work to owner-run businesses, built and run by Anuda Pasandul.

## Offer

**Service**:
One of the four things Loopstak sells: Website design, AI agents and automation, Claude integration, AI teaching.
_Avoid_: product, package, solution

**Website design**:
The Own-It Website build, a one-off price for a site of up to 5 pages, plus optional monthly Upkeep.
_Avoid_: web dev, site package

**Upkeep**:
The monthly plan that keeps a client's website updated and running.
_Avoid_: maintenance, retainer, care plan

**AI agents and automation**:
A monthly service that builds and runs software agents and automations for a client's business.
_Avoid_: AI Ops, bots

**Claude integration**:
A one-off job that connects Claude to a client's existing tools.
_Avoid_: AI partner, certified integration

**AI teaching**:
A paid session that teaches a client's team to use AI in their own work.
_Avoid_: workshop (fine in copy, not as the service name), training course

**Idea call**:
The free 30-minute call where we name three tasks AI or a better website could take over, with a rough price for each.
_Avoid_: consultation, discovery call, Google check

**Homepage mock-up**:
A free one-screen design of a prospect's new homepage, offered only after an Idea call to prospects who need a website.
_Avoid_: free design, sample

## Proof

**Client work**:
A live site or tool built for a paying client, shown with the client's permission.
_Avoid_: case study (fine as a page format), portfolio

**Own build**:
A working tool Anuda built for himself, shown as proof of skill and labelled as his own project.
_Avoid_: client project, product

**Friend build**:
A tool built for a friend, not paid, labelled as such.
_Avoid_: client work

## Ownership

**Keys**:
Every account, domain and login a client needs, held in the client's name from day one.
_Avoid_: handover, access
